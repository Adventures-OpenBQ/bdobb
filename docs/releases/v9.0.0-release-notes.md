# BDOBB v9.0.0 — Adventures in OpenBQ

BDOBB 9.0.0 is Episode 9's release: **EODHD data** as the stack's `openbq-eodhd` extension serves it, EODHD's calendars in the Calendar view, and the Episode 9 calendars example, on top of clean v8.0.0. It pairs with openbq-docker v9 (branch `claude/openbq-docker-v9`), whose openbb-api carries the `openbb-eodhd` extension. The assembly ledger is `docs/releases/v9-assembly.md`; per-file dispositions are in `docs/releases/v9-delta-manifest.tsv`; source provenance is in `docs/releases/provenance.md`.

Status: **released** as v9.0.0 by the 2026-10-16 regression walk; its gates, review and NAS checks are in `docs/releases/v9-ssrm-walk.md` and the v9.0.0 tag message.

## Local v9 chart expansion

The chart-expansion branch adds seven linked financial charts and section placement and ordering in the income Statement editor. The paired stack vendors the 53-mirror provider at commit `349f2de40773cf2e1d9e2993ebabf3184871c07d`, including the guarded earnings-versus-estimates route. The worked dashboard uses four fictional tables served from a local directory. See the [example and setup](../examples/v9-chart-expansion/README.md) and [verification record](validation/v9/chart-expansion/verification.md).

## Local v9 risk and ETF chart expansion

Four additional linked charts use the presented rows of one source table. **Return distribution** counts adjacent daily returns by interval. **Small multiples** compares adjusted prices on a common baseline and shared scales. **ETF holdings Pareto** ranks up to 50 holdings and discloses the full delivered weight and provider coverage. **ETF exposure** displays every reported sector or region, including zero weights. The native example includes the four charts and four fictional source tables. [Example and setup](../examples/v9-risk-exposure/README.md) · [Verification](validation/v9/risk-exposure/verification.md)

## Local v9 screening and income chart expansion

Three linked charts use one acquired table each. **Screening bubble** shows dividend yield versus five-day price change, with market cap area and an optional regression for the presented page and date cohort. **Dividend steps** shows observed split-adjusted distributions over a verified interval. **Income Sankey** reconciles one actual Statement period using its reporting currency and monetary scale. The paired EODHD routes support opt-in acquisition receipts. Live dividend coverage and income scale are currently unknown and those cases show repair guidance. The fictional dashboard includes explicit evidence for both positive cases and a server mode to try the refusals. [Example and setup](../examples/v9-screening-income/README.md)

## Local v9 news sentiment calendar

**News sentiment calendar** shows the daily mean polarity of delivered CompanyNews articles for one qualified instrument. It reports the acquired query, page, retrieval time, and the delivered and scored counts. Hover, tap, or open **Calendar day details** for original values. The view follows the latest acquired 12 months or a pinned window within the acquisition. Live use requires the paired EODHD CompanyNews route to return a verified receipt. The companion fictional example has a mode for trying the missing-evidence refusal. [Example and setup](../examples/v9-next-news-sentiment-calendar/README.md)

## Local v9 return summaries

**Return summaries** compares observed daily adjusted-price returns for up to twelve listings. Choose R7 boxes with observed whiskers and outliers, or Gaussian violins on a shared density scale. Use each listing's own dates or pin common inclusive bounds. The source must be a documented local adjusted-price snapshot or a table derived from it. Ordinary remote price responses cannot verify the data contract. [Synthetic example and local setup](../examples/v9-next-return-box-violin/README.md)

## Local v9 ownership snapshots

**Ownership snapshot** shows independent bars for acquired issuer measures, with source units and dates. A separately acquired, certified partition can be displayed as a 100-cell waffle with exact shares in the details. The opt-in EODHD ShareStatistics contract covers US primary common stock insider and institutional percentages. Additional direct measures and waffle require compatible acquired evidence. [Fictional examples and local setup](../examples/v9-next-ownership-snapshot/README.md)

**Linked metric** shows one acquired value with its verified units, measurement date or unknown status, vintage and retrieval time. An explicitly labeled user range enables a gauge, while values outside it stay visible on the card. The paired opt-in EODHD KeyMetrics and FinancialRatios routes verify reported trailing yield and trailing P/E for US primary common stock. The other four presets and acquired references require suitable local snapshots. [Fictional examples and local setup](../examples/v9-next-kpi-gauges/README.md)

## Local v9 ETF composition

**ETF sunburst** drills from one acquired fund into sectors and holdings. Hover or tap to see the original fund weight and the share of delivered holdings. Open the details for tiny or zero weights. The paired opt-in EODHD holdings route supports delivered holdings for qualified US ETFs. Live report dates, portfolio coverage and missing weight remain unknown. [Fictional example and local setup](../examples/v9-next-etf-sunburst/README.md)

## Local v9 historical overlays

**Dual-axis overlay** supports prepared local snapshots of adjusted closing price and reported diluted EPS, or US CPI-U and ten-year Treasury yield. Each series keeps its own dates and gaps on an independently scaled axis. EPS uses the report availability coordinate and discloses present-day restatements and acquisition vintages. CPI uses measurement month with release evidence when present. Both live recipes remain unavailable. [Synthetic snapshots and local setup](../examples/v9-next-dual-axis-overlays/README.md)

## What is new

- **EODHD data.** BDOBB needs no new code to show EODHD's widgets: the `openbq-eodhd` extension adds them to openbb-api's `widgets.json` (ids `eodhd_<route>_eodhd_obb`) — prices, the three statements, the economic and earnings calendars and the rest of the extension's namespace — and the widget library lists them. The key is the stack's `EODHD_API_KEY`; BDOBB holds none.
- **EODHD's calendars in the Calendar view.** The economic calendar filters with the Source → Release → Statistic tree, since its rows carry their publisher and release; the earnings calendar filters by Type and Series (`AAPL` keeps `AAPL.US`) and leads each symbol with its company logo on the desktop. The Calendar view itself is clean's since v4 and is unchanged.
- **Provider aggregation hints are kept.** A column's `aggregation` hint in `widgets.json` (which `openbq-eodhd` attaches to statement and price columns) now survives into the table's column model instead of being dropped; a malformed hint is dropped and the column kept. The Pivot drawer does not show the hint; a backend table too large for the table worker opens the older Pivot panel, which offers **Use suggested**. `arrange_table` applies the provider's operator when Rita leaves aggregation out, except across ungrouped instruments.
- **Help.** Quick Help gains **EODHD data**; the Reference Manual gains **EODHD Data** under *Integrations*; Calendar View and Example Dashboards point to them.

## Examples

- `docs/examples/v9.0.0-calendars.apps.json` — *Example Dashboard - Version 9.0.0 - Calendars*: on **Markets**, two EODHD price histories (table and chart) sharing a Symbol and an Interval group and EODHD's quarterly income statement in the Statement view; on **Calendars**, the EODHD U.S. economic calendar as a paged month, U.S. Treasury auction results as a vertical scroll of weeks, and the EODHD earnings calendar as a horizontal ribbon filtered to seven names. The first example that needs a paid key (EODHD's); the Treasury card is keyless. It opens in BDOBB 9.0.0 or later, and `scripts/apps-sync.mjs` routes it to openbb-api.

## Compatibility

- apps.json: no profile changes. The example's calendar and Statement state ride the existing v4/v5 extension.
- openbq-docker: the EODHD widgets exist only on a stack whose openbb-api carries `openbq-eodhd` (openbq-docker v9). Against openbq-docker v8 the example's EODHD cards are unresolved and the Treasury card still works. The web preview's gateway policy is unchanged (`openbqDocker` names the paired openbq-docker commit; the older `openbbDocker` key is historical).
