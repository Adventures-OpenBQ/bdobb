# BDOBB v9.0.0 — Adventures in OpenBQ

BDOBB 9.0.0 is Episode 9's release: **EODHD data** as the stack's `openbq-eodhd` extension serves it, EODHD's calendars in the Calendar view, and the Episode 9 calendars example, on top of clean v8.0.0. It pairs with openbq-docker v9 (branch `claude/openbq-docker-v9`), whose openbb-api carries the `openbb-eodhd` extension. The assembly ledger is `docs/releases/v9-assembly.md`; per-file dispositions are in `docs/releases/v9-delta-manifest.tsv`; source provenance is in `docs/releases/provenance.md`.

Status: **released** as v9.0.0 by the 2026-10-16 regression walk; its gates, review and NAS checks are in `docs/releases/v9-ssrm-walk.md` and the v9.0.0 tag message.

## What is new

- **EODHD data.** BDOBB needs no new code to show EODHD's widgets: the `openbq-eodhd` extension adds them to openbb-api's `widgets.json` (ids `eodhd_<route>_eodhd_obb`) — prices, the three statements, the economic and earnings calendars and the rest of the extension's namespace — and the widget library lists them. The key is the stack's `EODHD_API_KEY`; BDOBB holds none.
- **EODHD's calendars in the Calendar view.** The economic calendar filters with the Source → Release → Statistic tree, since its rows carry their publisher and release; the earnings calendar filters by Type and Series (`AAPL` keeps `AAPL.US`) and leads each symbol with its company logo on the desktop. The Calendar view itself is clean's since v4 and is unchanged.
- **Provider aggregation hints are kept.** A column's `aggregation` hint in `widgets.json` (which `openbq-eodhd` attaches to statement and price columns) now survives into the table's column model instead of being dropped; a malformed hint is dropped and the column kept. The Pivot drawer does not show the hint; a backend table too large for the table worker opens the older Pivot panel, which offers **Use suggested**. `arrange_table` applies the provider's operator when Rita leaves aggregation out, except across ungrouped instruments.
- **Help.** Quick Help gains **EODHD data**; the Reference Manual gains **EODHD Data** under *Integrations*; Calendar View and Example Dashboards point to them.

## Examples

- `docs/examples/v9.0.0-calendars.apps.json` — *Example Dashboard - Version 9.0.0 - Calendars*: on **Markets**, two EODHD price histories (table and chart) sharing a Symbol and an Interval group and EODHD's quarterly income statement in the Statement view; on **Calendars**, the EODHD U.S. economic calendar as a paged month, U.S. Treasury auction results as a vertical scroll of weeks, and the EODHD earnings calendar as a horizontal ribbon filtered to seven names. The first example that needs a paid key (EODHD's); the Treasury card is keyless. It opens in BDOBB 9.0.0 or later, and `scripts/apps-sync.mjs` routes it to openbb-api.

## Compatibility

- apps.json: no profile changes. The example's calendar and Statement state ride the existing v4/v5 extension.
- openbq-docker: the EODHD widgets exist only on a stack whose openbb-api carries `openbq-eodhd` (openbq-docker v9). Against openbq-docker v8 the example's EODHD cards are unresolved and the Treasury card still works. The web preview's gateway policy is unchanged (`openbqDocker` names the paired openbq-docker commit; the older `openbbDocker` key is historical).
