# Art's Charts

![An Art's Charts card](./shots/arts-charts.png)

From 12.0.0, BDOBB draws Art's Charts cards: charts kept as documents on the Art's
Charts server, which the card, Rita and snapshots share. A card follows every change
to its document within a second, the inbox in the left rail collects what watches and
schedules file, and Rita can read and change a chart when you ask her. This topic is
completed with the 12.0.0 guide.
