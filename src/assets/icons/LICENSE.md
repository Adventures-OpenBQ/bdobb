# Icons

Interface icons drawn from third-party artwork, each under its own licence.
The application icons, favicons and `src/assets/branding` are original BDOBB art and are not listed here.

## Pivot

`src/components/PivotIcon.tsx` draws **"Pivot" by Matt Garbarino, from the Noun
Project** — <https://thenounproject.com/icon/pivot-115905/> — licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0),
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>.

Changes made: cropped to the artwork (the download's two lines of credit text,
which sit beneath the drawing, cannot be read at the size a title bar draws
it), and drawn in the interface's text colour instead of black. The credit is given here, in `NOTICE`, in the generated
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (from `scripts/third-party-notices.mjs`), and in the
Pivot button's tooltip.

## Layout

The `workspace` glyph in `src/components/RailIcon.tsx`, drawn beside each
workspace in the left rail, is **"Layout" by M Yudi Maulana, from the Noun
Project** — <https://thenounproject.com/icon/layout-3135559/> — licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0),
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>.

Changes made: cropped to the artwork (the download's two lines of credit text,
which sit beneath the drawing, cannot be read at the size the rail draws it);
the three rectangles the source draws inside the block's openings left out, so
that the three panes are open rather than filled; and drawn in the interface's
text colour instead of black. The credit is given here, in `NOTICE`, and in the generated
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (from `scripts/third-party-notices.mjs`).

## AI

The `ai` glyph in `src/components/RailIcon.tsx`, drawn on the AI library row
of the left rail, is the **"ai" icon from Siemens Industrial Experience
icons** — <https://github.com/siemens/ix-icons>. Its path data is
byte-identical to `svg/ai.svg` in that repository's first commit (0c174f8881,
2022-09-20); upstream has since redrawn the icon, and ours is the 2022
drawing. It was obtained from SVG Repo, where it is listed as "Ai 40" in the
collection "Industrial Sharp UI Icons"
(<https://www.svgrepo.com/collection/industrial-sharp-ui-icons/>), author
Siemens, MIT License.

It is licensed under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 Siemens AG, which
requires that the copyright line and the permission notice accompany copies.
They are reproduced here, and the credit is also given in `NOTICE` and in the
generated `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (from `scripts/third-party-notices.mjs`).

Changes made: the source's wrapping groups reduced to the one
`translate(64 64)` group, the `<title>` and ids dropped, and drawn in the
interface's text colour instead of black.
